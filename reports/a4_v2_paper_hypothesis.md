# A4_v2 paper hypothesis

A3 encoder-only adaptation improved early MediaSpeech robustness (step-050) but did not preserve CV Scripted under its 10% replay design; later checkpoints weakened the robustness advantage while FLEURS was not the primary issue. A4 is not claimed to solve this trade-off. As an unchanged decoder-only diagnostic scope probe, it can test whether decoder adaptation changes the observed scope pattern. This remains a justified diagnostic hypothesis, subject to the unresolved validation-contract blocker.
